## 👋 About

I build **web applications, mobile apps, content management systems, and interactive digital solutions** — usually with React, Laravel, Flutter, and MySQL.

Most of my work is with **STARBOOKS** at **DOST-STII**, where I develop and maintain content management systems, administrative dashboards, kiosk interfaces, and offline-capable information platforms.
